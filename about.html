About Us

The world, reported straight.

Meridian News is an independent outlet covering international politics, business, science and culture. We report on events as they unfold across regions, without the noise of recycled wire copy.

Our goal is simple: give readers a clear, accurate picture of what's happening in the world — written and fact-checked in-house, not aggregated.

What we cover

Global politics and diplomacy, business and economic shifts, science and technology breakthroughs, and cultural stories that cross borders.

Editorial approach

Every article is written by our team and checked against primary sources before publishing. We use only licensed or neutral stock imagery — never unauthorized copyrighted material.
